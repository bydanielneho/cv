## Structure of the CVs

1. Profile
2. Key strengths
3. Leading and developing people (Channels and Digital Lead CV only)
4. Current role: Senior Advisor Communications, MCERT (1 July 2026 to current)
5. Previous roles: HUD Senior Advisor Communications (November 2024 to June 2026), then Office of the Ombudsman, Kāinga Ora and MSD
6. Other roles, certifications and referees

The CVs share the same roles. They differ in the profile, the key strengths and the order of sections within the MCERT and HUD roles. The Channels and Digital Lead CV adds a "Leading and developing people" section after Key strengths, because the role leads a team of five.
